# AI Matching Design (3-Level AI Face Clustering)

## Goal

Organize photos uploaded to a specific room into useful person collections using an anonymous 3-level visual face clustering pipeline. The AI groups visually similar faces without requiring mandatory face registration, selfie enrollment, or account-linked biometric identities.

## Terms

- **Face detection:** Locates faces within an uploaded photo and extracts bounding coordinates.
- **Face embedding:** Numeric vector generated by a deep learning model representing facial features for visual similarity comparison.
- **Anonymous person collection (Cluster):** A group of visually similar face instances clustered together automatically by the model within a room.
- **Cluster naming:** User-assigned human-readable label for an anonymous collection (e.g. naming "Person 1" as "Rafiqul" or "Samira"). The AI does not automatically know anyone's real-world identity.
- **Uncertain match suggestion:** A candidate similarity between two person collections or between an ambiguous face and an existing cluster, presented to the user for review.
- **Unsorted collection:** The fallback repository for photos lacking detectable faces, low-resolution faces, or ambiguous instances that cannot be grouped with high confidence.

The AI groups similar faces based purely on visual clustering; it does not identify people or link faces to registered user accounts.

## The 3-Level Architecture

### Level 1 — Confident Clustering
- Automatically group visually similar faces from uploaded photos into anonymous person collections (e.g., "Person 1", "Person 2").
- Operates strictly within the scope of the individual room using calibrated similarity thresholds.
- When a new photo is uploaded, faces that match an existing cluster with high confidence are automatically associated with that collection.
- If high-confidence faces appear that do not match existing clusters, a new anonymous person collection is created.
- Users and room members can name, rename, and edit these collections at any time.
- All automatic assignments preserve provenance metadata and can be undone or manually moved.

### Level 2 — Uncertain Matches
- When facial similarity or cluster distance falls within a marginal band, the system creates an *Uncertain Match* suggestion.
- Potential merges between two similar person clusters (or a borderline face and a cluster) are suggested for user review.
- **Never merge or assign automatically:** The system never combines uncertain person groups without explicit user confirmation.
- Users can review the suggestion with side-by-side thumbnail comparisons to either:
  1. **Confirm Merge:** Combine the two person collections into one.
  2. **Keep Separate (Dismiss):** Explicitly mark them as distinct individuals to prevent future repeat suggestions.

### Level 3 — Unsorted
- Photos that contain no detectable faces, severe motion blur, extreme occlusions, or faces that do not meet confidence thresholds remain in the **Unsorted** collection.
- Keeps room photos organized without forcing false assignments just to achieve a complete gallery.
- Users can browse the Unsorted collection and manually assign photos or individual face crops to an existing or new person collection, or leave them unsorted.

## Privacy & Biometric Safeguards

- **No Mandatory Face Registration or Selfies:** Users are never required to upload a selfie or register their face to use the application or join rooms.
- **Anonymous Grouping:** Clusters are created anonymously based solely on visual similarity within room photos. The system has zero knowledge of real-world identity until a user names a cluster.
- **Room Isolation:** Clustering and face embeddings are strictly scoped to the room where photos were uploaded. Embeddings are never cross-referenced across different rooms.
- **User Control & Deletion:** Room members can opt out of automatic clustering, manually correct any assignment, and room owners can purge all generated face clusters and vector embeddings at any time.

## Model Evaluation & Operational Requirements

- Calibration: Thresholds separating Level 1 (confident clustering), Level 2 (suggestions), and Level 3 (unsorted) are calibrated against diverse validation sets across lighting, poses, occlusions, and skin tones.
- Asynchronous Processing: Photo uploads complete immediately to private storage; clustering jobs run asynchronously via worker queues.
- Idempotency: Processing jobs are idempotent and safe to retry without creating duplicate clusters or conflicting assignments.
- Data Minimization: Raw images and embeddings are never written to application logs.
